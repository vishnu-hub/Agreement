A Mutual Understanding

Friends With Benefits

A few simple ground rules for keeping things fun, comfortable, respectful, and uncomplicated.

This isn't a legal contract. It's simply an understanding between two people who want to keep things honest and respectful.
Before acknowledging: Read everything properly. If there's anything you don't agree with or want changed, tick “I'd like to change something here” underneath that section and tell me what you'd prefer.

Nothing is assumed. We can discuss and change anything before agreeing.
01

Consent

  • Everything is mutual and consensual every time.
  • Either person can say no or stop at any moment.
  • No pressure, guilt-tripping, or assuming consent from previous occasions.
You don't need to phrase it perfectly. Just tell me what you mean.
02

Physical Intimacy

  • Kissing, cuddling, making out, and other mutually agreed intimacy are allowed.
  • Either person can initiate or decline.
  • Boundaries can be changed at any time.
03

Roleplay & Fantasies

  • Roleplay, fantasies, costumes, and agreed scenarios are allowed only when both are comfortable.
  • Discuss boundaries beforehand, especially anything new or potentially sensitive.
  • Either person can stop a scenario immediately.
04

Photos & Nudes

  • Intimate photos/videos may only be created or exchanged with explicit consent.
  • Never screenshot, save, forward, post, or show intimate content to anyone else without permission.
  • No recording during intimate moments unless both people explicitly agree beforehand.
  • Either person can ask for content to be deleted at any time, and the other person should respect the request.
05

Other People

  • Both people remain free to date or see others unless exclusivity is explicitly agreed.
  • No bringing another person into the arrangement without discussing it first.
  • Be honest about anything that could affect the other person's consent or sexual health.
06

Emotional Boundaries

  • This arrangement does not automatically mean a relationship.
  • No expectations of constant texting, dates, gifts, or relationship-level commitment.
  • If feelings develop, communicate honestly rather than becoming possessive or distant without explanation.
07

Privacy

  • What happens between us stays between us.
  • No gossiping, sharing intimate details, or showing messages/photos to friends.
  • No using private information against each other.
08

Communication

  • Talk openly about boundaries, comfort levels, protection, and expectations.
  • If something feels uncomfortable, say it.
  • No punishment or resentment for setting a boundary.
09

Ending the Arrangement

  • Either person can end the FWB arrangement at any time and for any reason.
  • No guilt, arguments, retaliation, or pressure.
  • After ending it, both respect each other's space and privacy.
The Golden Rule

“If it’s not clearly agreed upon, ask first. Consent, privacy, and respect come before everything else.”
